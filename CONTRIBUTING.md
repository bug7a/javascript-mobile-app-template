# Contributing

Thank you for your interest. This repository holds the ready copies of the templates: the files here are made by a script, so a change made here is lost at the next update.

**Please make your change in [js-components](https://github.com/bug7a/js-components/tree/main/04-templates/mobile-app-template).** The templates are developed there, next to basic.js and the components they use.

| You want to change | Where |
|---|---|
| A template (its pages, its look, its readme) | [`04-templates/mobile-app-template/`](https://github.com/bug7a/js-components/tree/main/04-templates/mobile-app-template) |
| A component (`Toggle`, `BottomBar`...) | [`comp/`](https://github.com/bug7a/js-components/tree/main/comp) |
| basic.js | [`basic/`](https://github.com/bug7a/js-components/tree/main/basic) |
| This README or this page | [`04-templates/mobile-app-template/_repo-files/`](https://github.com/bug7a/js-components/tree/main/04-templates/mobile-app-template/_repo-files) |

To report a problem, open an issue in [js-components](https://github.com/bug7a/js-components/issues).

Using a template for your own app needs none of this: copy its folder and change it as you like.
